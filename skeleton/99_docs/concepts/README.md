# concepts — Explaining concepts and domains

Documentation of what each thing is: business concepts, architecture, flows.

## What it is for

Letting someone (you or an agent) understand how something works before touching it.

## What goes here

- Explanation: what the concept is, what it's for.
- Concrete examples.
- Relationships to other concepts.
- Where it lives (in which code, service, database).

## What does NOT go here

- Plans (99_docs/plans).
- Decisions (99_docs/decisions).
- Step-by-step operation (runbooks).

## Naming

- `name.md`, no date (permanent, updated in place).
- Title at heading level 1.

Example:
```
# Client — who requests and pays

A client is a person or organization that...
```

## For AI agents

- Concepts are required reading before touching a domain.
- Before creating something new, read the concept first.
- If the concept is out of date, update it (don't copy-paste around it).
